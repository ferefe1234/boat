# Decimen Entity Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0 — August 2026

This is the entity counterpart of the [Individual CLA](CLA.md). It is signed
by a corporation or other legal entity (the "Entity") whose employees or
contractors submit Contributions to the Decimen projects —
decimen-optical-transfer, decimen-codec, and any other project owned or
managed by Evan Crawley (Bash Alarmist) (the "Maintainer") — in the course
of their work for the Entity, where the Entity owns or has rights in the
resulting work.

The context is the same as the Individual CLA's: the Projects are
dual-licensed (AGPL-3.0-or-later publicly, commercial licenses offered by the
Maintainer), the grants below are what make that lawful for the Entity's
Contributions, and Section 3 is the Maintainer's binding promise that every
Contribution also remains available under the AGPL.

## How to sign

An authorized representative of the Entity emails the Maintainer at
<bashalarmistcontact@gmail.com> a completed copy of this agreement stating:

- the Entity's full legal name, jurisdiction of organization, and address;
- the name, title, and email of the authorized signer;
- the initial list of GitHub usernames designated to submit Contributions on
  the Entity's behalf.

The Entity must keep the designated-username list current by emailing
updates; Contributions from designated usernames are covered by this
agreement from the date of signature. On receipt, the Maintainer records the
signature and adds the designated usernames to the CLA workflow's allowlist —
that is how the automated status check recognizes entity-covered
contributors, who do not post the individual signing comment.
